# Purpose

Build a living evidence-based energy-sector intelligence brain that compounds as filings, technical reports, policy documents, and market analyses arrive.

It supports:
- **Executive decisions** — technology portfolio allocation, market entry, project FID
- **Business development** — PPA negotiation, partnership structuring, offtake agreement analysis
- **Product development** — technology benchmarking, LCOE optimization, component sourcing
- **Industrialization** — manufacturing scale, supply chain resilience, workforce planning
- **Investment screening** — policy risk assessment, technology cost curve analysis, infrastructure bottleneck identification

Every material conclusion must identify whether it is directly supported, calculated, inferred, hypothetical, conflicted, or unknown. Outputs explain mechanisms, confidence, counterevidence, and practical implications.
