# Purpose

Build a living evidence-based advanced-materials intelligence brain that compounds as filings, patents, materials science literature, and industry reports arrive.

It supports:
- **Executive decisions** — materials portfolio strategy, vertical integration, partnership evaluation
- **Business development** — application development, qualification pipeline, customer specification alignment
- **Product development** — property-performance mapping, processing optimization, substitute evaluation
- **Industrialization** — manufacturing process scale-up, yield optimization, quality certification
- **Investment screening** — technology maturity assessment, IP moat evaluation, supply chain risk analysis

Every material conclusion must identify whether it is directly supported, calculated, inferred, hypothetical, conflicted, or unknown. Outputs explain mechanisms, confidence, counterevidence, and practical implications.
