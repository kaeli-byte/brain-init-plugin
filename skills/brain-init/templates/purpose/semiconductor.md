# Purpose

Build a living evidence-based semiconductor-industry intelligence brain that compounds as filings, patents, technical papers, and market analyses arrive.

It supports:
- **Executive decisions** — fab investment, node transitions, foundry partner selection
- **Business development** — IP licensing, design-win tracking, competitive positioning
- **Product development** — architecture benchmarking, PPA optimization, packaging strategy
- **Industrialization** — yield management, equipment qualification, process technology
- **Investment screening** — technology moat durability, node roadmap credibility, equipment supplier leverage

Every material conclusion must identify whether it is directly supported, calculated, inferred, hypothetical, conflicted, or unknown. Outputs explain mechanisms, confidence, counterevidence, and practical implications.
