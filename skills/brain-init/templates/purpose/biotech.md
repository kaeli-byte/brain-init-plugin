# Purpose

Build a living evidence-based biotech/pharma intelligence brain that compounds as filings, clinical trial data, patents, scientific literature, and regulatory decisions arrive.

It supports:
- **Executive decisions** — pipeline prioritization, therapeutic area focus, partnership strategy
- **Business development** — in-licensing/out-licensing evaluation, M&A target screening
- **Product development** — clinical trial design, endpoint selection, CMC strategy
- **Industrialization** — manufacturing scale-up, supply chain qualification, quality systems
- **Investment screening** — probability of technical/regulatory success, commercial opportunity sizing, patent cliff exposure

Every material conclusion must identify whether it is directly supported, calculated, inferred, hypothetical, conflicted, or unknown. Outputs explain mechanisms, confidence, counterevidence, and practical implications.
